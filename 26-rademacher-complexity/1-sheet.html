Rademacher Complexity

Teacher's sheet · Lesson text (draft)

Overview

Objective: after this, you can define the Rademacher complexity of a set of vectors and of a loss class on a training sequence, compute it exactly for small sets and by Monte Carlo for large ones, prove the symmetrization lemma and McDiarmid’s inequality, state and prove the three Rademacher generalization bounds, use the four calculus rules (shift and scale, convex hull, Massart, contraction), bound the complexity of ℓ2\ell_2ℓ2​- and ℓ1\ell_1ℓ1​-bounded linear classes, and derive the norm-based SVM, hard-SVM, learned-norm and ℓ1\ell_1ℓ1​ bounds, none of which depend on the number of features.

Misconception: “the Rademacher complexity is a property of the class alone”. Why wrong: it is defined on a training sequence; the same 50,000-word halfspaces have VC dimension 50,000 and measured ℓ2\ell_2ℓ2​-ball complexity about 0.19 on 400 short messages.

Second: “smallest training risk gives the best certificate”. Why wrong: act 2’s B=4B = 4B=4 has ramp risk 0.008 but certificate 0.2021; B=2B = 2B=2 certifies 0.1396.

Third: “choosing among classes after looking costs nothing”. Why wrong: the bound is for a fixed class; a menu of eight needs δ/8\delta/8δ/8 each and a union bound (same device as the learned-norm theorem and lesson 7a’s SRM).

Fourth: “ℓ1\ell_1ℓ1​ always wins for sparse predictors”. Why wrong: it trades ∥x∥2\|\mathbf x\|_2∥x∥2​ for ∥x∥∞2log⁡2d\|\mathbf x\|_\infty\sqrt{2\log 2d}∥x∥∞​2log2d​; for 25-word messages and 10 key words the ℓ2\ell_2ℓ2​ term is 0.0707 vs 0.2146.

Problems (written for this lesson; Chapter 26 has no exercises; presented in the prose as the lesson’s own; hints one at a time, never a solution):

  • Problem 1 (thresholds by Massart).
    1. Sort the m scores. Between two consecutive scores, does the loss vector change as the threshold moves?
    2. So there are at most m + 1 distinct loss vectors (one per gap, including the two ends). Apply Massart with N = m + 1.
    3. Every entry is in [0, 1], and so is the mean’s; bound ∥a−aˉ∥\|\mathbf a - \bar{\mathbf a}\|∥a−aˉ∥ by m\sqrt mm​.

  • Problem 2 (one-sided confidence).
    1. Which direction of deviation does part 1 actually use?
    2. Rerun Step 5 of McDiarmid’s proof for f−Eff - \mathbb Eff−Ef only, and set e−2t2/(mc2)=δe^{-2t^2/(mc^2)} = \deltae−2t2/(mc2)=δ.
