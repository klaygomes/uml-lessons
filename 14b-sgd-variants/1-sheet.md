Terms:
- projection onto \(\mathcal H\): the closest point of \(\mathcal H\), \(\operatorname{argmin}_{\mathbf w \in \mathcal H}\|\mathbf w - \mathbf w^{(t+\frac12)}\|\); for a ball, \(\mathbf w \min(1, B/\|\mathbf w\|)\).
- projection lemma: for closed convex \(\mathcal H\), \(\mathbf v\) the projection of \(\mathbf w\), and every \(\mathbf u \in \mathcal H\), \(\|\mathbf w - \mathbf u\|^2 \ge \|\mathbf v - \mathbf u\|^2\).
- \(\lambda\)-strongly convex: \(f(\alpha\mathbf w + (1-\alpha)\mathbf u) \le \alpha f(\mathbf w) + (1-\alpha) f(\mathbf u) - \frac\lambda2\alpha(1-\alpha)\|\mathbf w - \mathbf u\|^2\).
- strongly convex rate: with \(\eta_t = 1/(\lambda t)\), projection and \(\mathbb E\|\mathbf v_t\|^2 \le \rho^2\), \(\mathbb E[f(\bar{\mathbf w})] - f(\mathbf w^\star) \le \rho^2(1 + \log T)/(2\lambda T)\).
- \(\beta\)-smooth: \(\|\nabla f(\mathbf v) - \nabla f(\mathbf w)\| \le \beta\|\mathbf v - \mathbf w\|\); self-bounded: \(\|\nabla f\|^2 \le 2\beta f\) for nonnegative smooth \(f\).
- SGD for RLM: draw \(z\) uniformly from \(S\), step along \(\lambda\mathbf w^{(t)} + \mathbf v_t\) with \(\eta_t = 1/(\lambda t)\); closed form \(\mathbf w^{(t+1)} = -\frac{1}{\lambda t}\sum_{i \le t}\mathbf v_i\); step vectors at most \(2\rho\); gap at most \(2\rho^2(1 + \log T)/(\lambda T)\).

Misconception: “projecting the iterates breaks the guarantee.” · Why wrong: projection onto a closed convex set never moves you further from \(\mathbf w^\star \in \mathcal H\), so the per-step identity becomes an inequality in the right direction.
Second misconception: “any shrinking step size gets the \(\log(T)/T\) rate.” · Why wrong: it needs strong convexity and \(\eta_t = 1/(\lambda t)\) with a \(\lambda\) no larger than the true one; claiming more curvature (\(\lambda' > \lambda\)) leaves terms \((\lambda' - \lambda)a_t/2 \ge 0\) after the telescoping, and \(B/(\rho\sqrt t)\) on a convex function gives only order \(1/\sqrt T\).
Third: “the fast rate means SGD learns from fewer fresh messages.” · It is an optimization rate for a strongly convex objective; the true-risk count for convex-Lipschitz-bounded problems stays \(B^2\rho^2/\varepsilon^2\).
Fourth: “the measured gap is far below the certificate, so fewer steps would do.” · The measurement is one world; the certificate holds for every training sequence.

1. Example: \(\mathcal H = \{(1, 0), (-1, 0)\}\) (or the unit circle \(\{\|\mathbf w\| = 1\}\)), \(\mathbf w = (0.1, 0)\), projection \(\mathbf v = (1, 0)\), \(\mathbf u = (-1, 0) \in \mathcal H\): \(\|\mathbf w - \mathbf u\| = 1.1\) but \(\|\mathbf v - \mathbf u\| = 2\). In the step lemma, \(\|\mathbf w^{(t+1)} - \mathbf w^\star\|^2 \le \|\mathbf w^{(t+\frac12)} - \mathbf w^\star\|^2\) can fail, so the per-step inequality and the telescoping (and with them the guarantee) are lost. 2. \(\mathbf w^{(2)} = -\frac{1}{0.1}(0.1, 0.9) = (-1, -9)\) (D at \(\mathbf 0\) has signed score 0, so \(\mathbf v_1 = -y\mathbf x = (0.1, 0.9)\)). End-of-chapter problems (Section 14.8, PDF p. 201). In the prose they appear as the lesson’s Problems 1 and 2: Problem 1 = Exercise 1, Problem 2 = Exercise 4 (starred). Hints one rung at a time; never the solution.
- Exercise 1 (Claim 14.10), standard.
  1. Apply strong convexity to the pair \(\mathbf u, \mathbf w\) at the point \(\mathbf w + \alpha(\mathbf u - \mathbf w) = \alpha\mathbf u + (1-\alpha)\mathbf w\): \(f(\mathbf w + \alpha(\mathbf u - \mathbf w)) \le \alpha f(\mathbf u) + (1-\alpha)f(\mathbf w) - \frac\lambda2\alpha(1-\alpha)\|\mathbf w - \mathbf u\|^2\).
  2. Lower-bound the left side with the subgradient inequality at \(\mathbf w\): \(f(\mathbf w + \alpha(\mathbf u - \mathbf w)) \ge f(\mathbf w) + \alpha\langle \mathbf u - \mathbf w, \mathbf v \rangle\).
  3. Combine, cancel \(f(\mathbf w)\), divide by \(\alpha > 0\), let \(\alpha \to 0\): \(\langle \mathbf u - \mathbf w, \mathbf v \rangle \le f(\mathbf u) - f(\mathbf w) - \frac\lambda2\|\mathbf w - \mathbf u\|^2\). Multiply by \(-1\).
- Exercise 4 (variable step size), starred.
  1. Redo the step lemma’s per-step identity with \(\eta_t\): \(\langle \mathbf w^{(t)} - \mathbf w^\star, \mathbf v_t \rangle \le \frac{a_t - a_{t+1}}{2\eta_t} + \frac{\eta_t}{2}\|\mathbf v_t\|^2\) (inequality with projection).
  2. The sum \(\sum_t (a_t - a_{t+1})/(2\eta_t)\) no longer telescopes. Rearrange (summation by parts): \(\le \frac{a_1}{2\eta_1} + \sum_{t=2}^T a_t\left(\frac{1}{2\eta_t} - \frac{1}{2\eta_{t-1}}\right)\); the brackets are positive.
  3. You need \(a_t\) bounded: that is why the projection onto the ball of radius \(B\) is there, giving \(a_t \le (2B)^2\). The sum collapses to at most \(4B^2/(2\eta_T) = 2B\rho\sqrt T\).
  4. \(\sum_t \eta_t\rho^2/2 = \frac{B\rho}{2}\sum_t t^{-1/2} \le B\rho\sqrt T\) (compare with \(\int_0^T s^{-1/2}ds\)). Total \(3B\rho\sqrt T\); divide by \(T\); then the expectation argument of the SGD guarantee: \(\mathbb E[f(\bar{\mathbf w})] - f(\mathbf w^\star) \le 3B\rho/\sqrt T\).
- Exercise 3 went to lesson 14a. Not chosen: Exercise 2 (Corollary 14.14), because its proof is written out in “Smooth losses”; offer it as a self-check without reading the proof.
