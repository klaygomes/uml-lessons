Overview

Objective: after this, you can explain why the representation is prior knowledge and why a feature’s quality depends on the learner; state feature selection as min⁡LS(w)\min L_S(\mathbf w)minLS​(w) s.t. ∥w∥0≤k\|\mathbf w\|_0 \le k∥w∥0​≤k and say why it is solved approximately; derive the filter-method score for linear regression (best single-feature line, Pearson’s ρ\rhoρ) and show a case where it fails; run forward greedy selection, prove that OMP makes the same choices with the new-part score ⟨uj,y⟩2/∥uj∥2\langle \mathbf u_j, \mathbf y \rangle^2/\|\mathbf u_j\|^2⟨uj​,y⟩2/∥uj​∥2, derive the steepest-coordinate rule from β\betaβ-smoothness, and describe backward elimination; prove soft thresholding and explain why the ℓ1\ell_1ℓ1​ price gives exact zeros where ℓ2\ell_2ℓ2​ does not; solve the LASSO by coordinate descent; show how normalization and clipping change what ridge and linear regression can do, and apply the toolbox of transformations; describe auto-encoders, kkk-means and sparse coding as feature learning; and certify a few-feature mail filter with the finite-class uniform-convergence bound.

Derivation (all in the prose with proofs, except as noted):

  • Filter-method score: centering equality (deferred to Problem 1, checked numerically), b=0b = 0b=0 from centered sums, a=⟨⟩/∥∥2a = \langle\rangle/\|\|^2a=⟨⟩/∥∥2, minimum value, ranking by ∣ρ∣|\rho|∣ρ∣, ∣ρ∣≤1|\rho| \le 1∣ρ∣≤1 by Cauchy–Schwarz.
  • Filter failure: E[yx1]=0\mathbb E[yx_1] = 0E[yx1​]=0 by linearity and independence.
  • OMP proposition: same span, expand, ⟨u,Vθ⟩=0\langle \mathbf u, V\boldsymbol\theta \rangle = 0⟨u,Vθ⟩=0 from V⊤V=IV^\top V = IV⊤V=I, separate minimization, parabola in α\alphaα.
  • Steepest coordinate: one-dimensional quadratic upper bound from β\betaβ-smoothness (lesson 12a), minimized at η=−∂jR/β\eta = -\partial_j R/\betaη=−∂j​R/β.
  • Penalized ⇒ budgeted with k1=∥wλ∥1k_1 = \|\mathbf w_\lambda\|_1k1​=∥wλ​∥1​ (proved); converse via Lagrange duality (stated, not proved).
  • Soft thresholding: three cases. One-dimensional LASSO by reduction; ℓ2\ell_2ℓ2​ closed form by derivative.
  • LASSO coordinate update by reduction to soft thresholding (convergence not proved).
  • Ridge closed form by gradient, invertibility; objective comparison; clipping w⋆=(2a−1)/(a2+a−1)w^\star = (2a - 1)/(a^2 + a - 1)w⋆=(2a−1)/(a2+a−1).
  • Linear predictor on one-hot cluster codes is constant per cluster.
  • Certificate: lesson 4’s two-sided uniform convergence for finite classes, union over kkk with δk=δ/2k\delta_k = \delta/2^kδk​=δ/2k.
