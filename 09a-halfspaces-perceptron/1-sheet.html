Overview

Misconception: “the Perceptron needs about (RB)2(RB)^2(RB)2 updates”. Why wrong: it is an upper bound over every order; on the eight messages the bound is 142.3 and the run takes 3.

Second misconception: “any separator certifies (RB)2(RB)^2(RB)2”. Why wrong: a separator u\mathbf uu certifies (R/γu)2≥(RB)2(R/\gamma_{\mathbf u})^2 \ge (RB)^2(R/γu​)2≥(RB)2; (−1,2,−2)(-1, 2, -2)(−1,2,−2) certifies 189, (−0.5,1.5,−1)(-0.5, 1.5, -1)(−0.5,1.5,−1) certifies 294.

Third misconception: “halfspaces are always easy to learn”. Why wrong: agnostic ERM for halfspaces is computationally hard (book p. 119, Ben-David and Simon 2001); hence surrogate losses (lesson 9b’s logistic regression).

Fourth misconception: “the bias term is the inductive bias”.
