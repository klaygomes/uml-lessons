Regularization and Stability

Teacher's sheet · Lesson text (draft)

Overview

Objective: after this, you can explain why ERM is unstable when the empirical risk has a nearly flat valley; define regularized loss minimization and Tikhonov regularization and read RLM as SRM over nested norm balls; derive and solve ridge regression and prove its system is invertible and its solution unique; state and prove that the expected overfitting gap equals the expected replace-one change; define on-average-replace-one stability; split the expected production error into expected fit plus expected replace-one change; and choose λ\lambdaλ so that both averages meet a target.

Misconception: "regularization is good because small weights are good". Why wrong: the penalty adds curvature 2λ2\lambda2λ in every direction; it matters in the flat valley (0.0019 to 0.2019) and barely in steep directions (0.814 to 1.014); what it buys is stability, which equals a bound on overfitting.

Second: "a stable learner is a good learner". Why wrong: the constant learner 0\mathbf 00 has rate 0 and production error 0.5; in act 2, λ=1\lambda = 1λ=1 has replace-one change 0.0092 but expected production error 0.413. Third (new in the split): "stability is only a hint that a learner generalizes". Why wrong: on average it is an identity: 0.128 = 0.128 in the one-word world; fit plus change 0.424 against formula 0.425 for ERM in the campaign world (the “Fix the bug” snippet, seed 1).

Derivation:

  • Ridge: gradient 2λw+1m∑(⟨w,xi⟩−yi)xi2\lambda\mathbf w + \frac1m\sum(\langle\mathbf w, \mathbf x_i\rangle - y_i)\mathbf x_i2λw+m1​∑(⟨w,xi​⟩−yi​)xi​; invertibility via v⊤(2λmI+A)v≥2λm∥v∥2\mathbf v^\top(2\lambda mI + A)\mathbf v \ge 2\lambda m\|\mathbf v\|^2v⊤(2λmI+A)v≥2λm∥v∥2 (the book: "eigenvalues bounded below by 2λm2\lambda m2λm"); exact expansion shows uniqueness (ours).
  • Theorem 13.2: book's two-line proof; ours spells out conditioning on SSS and the exchange of positions iii and m+1m+1m+1 of an i.i.d. sequence.
  • Split of the production error (Fix the bug): add and subtract LS(A(S))L_S(A(S))LS​(A(S)), then Theorem 13.2; the book writes it as Equation 13.15 in §13.4 (lesson 13b).
