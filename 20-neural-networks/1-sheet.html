Misconception: “one hidden layer computes anything, so size doesn’t matter”. Why wrong: universality on nnn bits needs ∣E∣≥2n/(n+log⁡2e)|E| \ge 2^n/(n + \log_2 e)∣E∣≥2n/(n+log2​e) edges.

Second: “label cost grows with neurons”. Why wrong: it grows with edges, O(∣E∣log⁡∣E∣)O(|E|\log|E|)O(∣E∣log∣E∣).

Third: “the VC bound covers sigmoids”. Why wrong: the proof needs finitely many output values per neuron; real-weight sigmoid networks can reach Ω(∣E∣2)\Omega(|E|^2)Ω(∣E∣2); bbb-bit weights give b∣E∣b|E|b∣E∣.

Fourth: “SGD finds the best weights”. Why wrong: nonconvex loss; ERM NP-hard even for 3 hidden neurons.

Fifth: “zero initialisation is fine”. Why wrong: symmetric neurons stay identical (snippet: three identical rows after 2,000 steps).

Sixth: “choosing the best of several runs is cheating”. Why wrong: the certificate is uniform over the class.
