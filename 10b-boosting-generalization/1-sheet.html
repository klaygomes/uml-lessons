Misconception: "more rounds means a better filter". Why wrong: the class and its VC dimension grow with TTT; certified risk 0.175 at 40 rounds, 0.227 at 100.

Second misconception: "the theorem says AdaBoost learns". Why wrong: it bounds the empirical risk only; fresh mail needs the size of L(B,T)L(B, T)L(B,T).

Third misconception: "the certified risk is what the filter will score on fresh mail". Why wrong: it is a ceiling for every distribution and every vote in the class; at 80 rounds 0.203 against a fresh error of 0.006.
