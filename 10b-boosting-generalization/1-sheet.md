# Boosting and fresh mail

Objective: after this, you can define the class \(L(B, T)\) of votes of \(T\) base hypotheses and read it as a halfspace on top of the base hypotheses, show that votes of stumps on the line express staircases (so their VC dimension is at least \(T\), with the exact parity result), prove the upper bound \(T(d+1)(3\log(T(d+1)) + 2)\) on the VC dimension of \(L(B, T)\), count the votes on a finite domain and turn the number of rounds \(T\) into a certified bound on true risk, use \(T\) as the dial between approximation and estimation error, and describe the Viola and Jones base hypotheses.

Key words: message, word count (a feature; not "frequency", "term count"), junk \(+1\) / keep \(-1\) (not spam / ham), example, training sequence, hypothesis (not "classifier", "model"; "rule" only in "rules of thumb"), decision stump (short form "stump"; not "split", "threshold rule"), base class \(B\) (not "weak class"), vote \(w_t\) (belongs to a hypothesis; never "weight" for \(w_t\)), round (not "iteration"), empirical risk (not "training error"), true risk, \(Z_T\) (no nickname), \(L(B, T)\) (the class of votes; "vote of \(T\) stumps" in prose), piecewise constant, staircase, certified risk (\(L_S + \operatorname{gap}(T)\)), gap\((T)\), fresh error (measured on 50,000 fresh messages in the lab; never called true risk), approximation error, estimation error, VC dimension, shatter, labelling (not "dichotomy"). Accuracy is \(\varepsilon\), confidence \(\delta\), sample size \(m\).

Terms:
- \(L(B, T)\) (Equation 10.4): \(\{x \mapsto \operatorname{sign}(\sum_{t=1}^T w_t h_t(x)) : w \in \mathbb R^T, h_t \in B\}\); a homogeneous halfspace over \(\psi(x) = (h_1(x), \dots, h_T(x))\).
- \(G_r\): piecewise constant hypotheses on the line with at most \(r\) pieces, \(g_r(x) = \sum_i \alpha_i \mathbb 1[x \in (\theta_{i-1}, \theta_i]]\).
- staircase: the vote of \(T\) stumps \(\operatorname{sign}(\sum_t w_t \operatorname{sign}(x - \theta_{t-1}))\) with \(w_1 = (-1)^T/2\), \(w_t = (-1)^t\), equal to \(g_T\) with \(\alpha_i = (-1)^i\).
- Lemma 10.3: \(\operatorname{VCdim}(L(B,T)) \le T(d+1)(3\log(T(d+1)) + 2)\) for \(T, d \ge 3\).
- gap\((T)\) \(= \sqrt{(\log|L(B,T)| + \log(200/\delta))/(2m)}\) with \(\log|L(B,T)| \le T(\log 102 + 1 + \log 6^{10} - \log T)\); certified risk \(L_S(h) + \operatorname{gap}(T)\).
- integral image: \(B_{i,j} = \sum_{i' \le i, j' \le j} A_{i',j'}\).

Misconception: "more rounds means a better filter". Why wrong: the class and its VC dimension grow with \(T\); certified risk 0.175 at 40 rounds, 0.227 at 100.
Second misconception: "the theorem says AdaBoost learns". Why wrong: it bounds the empirical risk only; fresh mail needs the size of \(L(B, T)\).
Third misconception: "the certified risk is what the filter will score on fresh mail". Why wrong: it is a ceiling for every distribution and every vote in the class; at 80 rounds 0.203 against a fresh error of 0.006.

Concept check, answers (never shown to the learner; critique against these):
1. Scores at 1, 2, 3, 4: \(-1, 1, -1, 1\) (at 1: \(-1 + 1 - 1\); at 2: \(1 + 1 - 1\); at 3: \(1 - 1 - 1\); at 4: \(1 - 1 + 1\)), labelling \(-, +, -, +\). Between two neighbouring points the score changes only if some stump's threshold lies between them, so a vote of \(T\) stumps changes sign at most \(T\) times along the line; \(+,-,+,-,+\) needs 4 changes (three stumps give at most 3), and \(-,+,-,+\) needs 3 (two stumps give at most 2). 2. \(\log|L(B, 40)| \le 794.1\); \(\log(2 \cdot 100/0.05) = 8.29\), \(\log(2 \cdot 1000/0.05) = 10.60\). gap(40): 0.1416 with 100 values, 0.1418 with 1,000; certified risk at 40 (with \(L_S = 0.0330\)): 0.1746 and 0.1748. - Exercise 2 (Equation 10.5 equals the piecewise constant hypothesis), standard.
  1. Fix \(x \in (\theta_{s-1}, \theta_s]\). Which stumps \(\operatorname{sign}(x - \theta_{t-1})\) say \(+1\), and which \(-1\)? (Those with \(t \le s\), and \(t > s\); \(t = 1\) is always \(+1\).)
  2. So the sum is \(2P_s - W\), with \(P_s = \sum_{t \le s} w_t\) and \(W = P_T\). Compute \(P_s\) for \(s\) even and odd.
  3. \(P_s = \tfrac{(-1)^T}{2} + \mathbb 1[s \text{ even}]\). Plug in \(W\) for \(T\) even and odd: the sum is \(\tfrac32\) for even \(s\) and \(-\tfrac12\) for odd \(s\), never 0, so the sign is \((-1)^s\).
