Misconception: "AdaBoost's empirical risk goes down every round". Why wrong: on the ten messages it stays at 0.2 after round 2; only ZtZ_tZt​ is guaranteed to fall.

Second misconception: "more rounds means a better filter". Why wrong: the class and its VC dimension grow with TTT; certified risk 0.175 at 40 rounds, 0.227 at 100.

Third misconception: "a weak learner is a weak assumption". Why wrong: the edge must hold on every reweighting, which is what each round of the proof uses.
