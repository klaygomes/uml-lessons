Overview

Objective: after this, you can state the online protocol and explain why no guarantee from lessons 2 to 20 survives an adaptive adversary; define mistake bounds and online learnability; prove the mistake bounds of Consistent (∣H∣−1|\mathcal H| - 1∣H∣−1) and Halving (log⁡2∣H∣\log_2|\mathcal H|log2​∣H∣); define shattered trees and the Littlestone dimension, prove the tree lower bound, the root split and SOA’s bound, and so the exact characterization MSOA(H)=Ldim(H)M_{\text{SOA}}(\mathcal H) = \mathrm{Ldim}(\mathcal H)MSOA​(H)=Ldim(H); prove VCdim≤Ldim\mathrm{VCdim} \le \mathrm{Ldim}VCdim≤Ldim with an infinite gap; define regret and prove Cover’s impossibility for deterministic learners; prove Weighted-Majority’s regret 2log⁡(d)T\sqrt{2\log(d)T}2log(d)T​ step by step and turn it into regret bounds for finite classes and for classes of finite Littlestone dimension; prove Online Gradient Descent’s regret BρTB\rho\sqrt TBρT​; derive the online Perceptron from OGD on round-dependent hinge surrogates and prove its mistake bound; and give each learner’s time and space per round.

Bug: the spammers adapt after every decision the filter makes; mail arrives one message at a time with no fixed distribution, so every guarantee of lessons 2 to 20 (all assume S∼DmS \sim \mathcal D^mS∼Dm) is void, and the nightly retrain loses every morning.

Fix: online learning. Act 2 of the lab: each week the learner chooses an online learner and its parameters against an adversary that plays optimally (or, for the expert weeks, punishes the learner’s most trusted experts); the week ships only if the proved bound with the chosen parameters (mistake bound, regret bound or Perceptron certificate) is within the week’s budget.

Terms:

  • round: one pass of receive xtx_txt​, predict ptp_tpt​, see yty_tyt​, update.
  • online learner: an algorithm that predicts each round from the past rounds only.
  • adversary: whoever chooses the messages (and, without realizability, the labels), possibly after seeing all past predictions.
  • realizable sequence: some h⋆∈Hh^\star \in \mathcal Hh⋆∈H has yt=h⋆(xt)y_t = h^\star(x_t)yt​=h⋆(xt​) for all ttt.
  • mistake bound: MA(H)=sup⁡S∣{t:pt≠yt}∣≤B<∞M_A(\mathcal H) = \sup_S |\{t : p_t \neq y_t\}| \le B < \inftyMA​(H)=supS​∣{t:pt​=yt​}∣≤B<∞ over realizable SSS; online learnable: some AAA has one.
  • version space VtV_tVt​: the hypotheses consistent with rounds 1,…,t−11, \dots, t-11,…,t−1.
  • shattered tree: complete binary tree of depth ddd of messages such that every label path is followed by some h∈Hh \in \mathcal Hh∈H.
  • Littlestone dimension: the largest depth of a shattered tree (∞\infty∞ if unbounded).
  • regret: sup⁡\supsup over sequences and h∈Hh \in \mathcal Hh∈H of the learner’s mistakes (expected, if randomized) minus hhh’s.
  • expert / cost: an adviser; vt,i∈[0,1]v_{t,i} \in [0,1]vt,i​∈[0,1] is what following expert iii costs on round ttt.
  • online convex optimization: convex H⊆Rd\mathcal H \subseteq \mathbb R^dH⊆Rd, ℓ(⋅,z)\ell(\cdot, z)ℓ(⋅,z) convex, the learner commits w(t)\mathbf w^{(t)}w(t) before ztz_tzt​ is revealed.

Misconception: “online learning is batch learning one example at a time.” · Why wrong: what changes is who chooses the examples; thresholds have VC dimension 1 (easy in batch) and Littlestone dimension ∞\infty∞ (impossible online).

Second: “any consistent hypothesis will do.” · Consistent can be forced into ∣H∣−1|\mathcal H| - 1∣H∣−1 mistakes, Halving at most log⁡2∣H∣\log_2|\mathcal H|log2​∣H∣.

Third: “randomizing only adds noise.” · Without it, regret T/2T/2T/2 is forced even for two constant hypotheses (Cover).

Fourth: “WM’s bound needs random costs.” · It holds for every cost sequence; the expectation is only over the learner’s coin.

Fifth: “the Perceptron learns any separable stream with few mistakes.” · Only with margin; Ldim of halfspaces is infinite for d≥2d \ge 2d≥2.
