Second misconception: "with enough data, true risk goes to zero". Why wrong: the Bayes risk (0.125 here) is a floor under every hypothesis when labels disagree.

Third misconception: "minimizing empirical risk is the goal". Why wrong: ERM over all 65,536 hypotheses gets LS=1/12L_S = 1/12LS​=1/12 on the worked example and true risk 0.35, double hFh_FhF​'s 0.175.
