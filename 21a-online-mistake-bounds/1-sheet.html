Online Mistake Bounds

Teacher's sheet · Lesson text (draft)

Overview

Objective: after this, you can state the online protocol and explain why no guarantee from lessons 2 to 20 survives an adaptive adversary; define mistake bounds and online learnability; prove the mistake bounds of Consistent (∣H∣−1|\mathcal H| - 1∣H∣−1) and Halving (log⁡2∣H∣\log_2|\mathcal H|log2​∣H∣); define shattered trees and the Littlestone dimension, prove the tree lower bound, the root split and SOA’s bound, and so the exact characterization MSOA(H)=Ldim(H)M_{\text{SOA}}(\mathcal H) = \mathrm{Ldim}(\mathcal H)MSOA​(H)=Ldim(H); prove VCdim≤Ldim\mathrm{VCdim} \le \mathrm{Ldim}VCdim≤Ldim with an infinite gap; and give each learner’s time and space per round.

Bug: the spammers adapt after every decision the filter makes; mail arrives one message at a time with no fixed distribution, so every guarantee of lessons 2 to 20 (all assume S∼DmS \sim \mathcal D^mS∼Dm) is void, and the nightly retrain loses every morning.

Fix: online learning with a proved mistake bound. Act 2 of the lab (new in the split, spec below): three realizable weeks, each with a class and a mistake budget; the learner chooses Consistent, Halving or SOA, and the week ships only if the learner’s proved mistake bound on that class is within the budget. The adversary then plays optimally and the realized count, never above the bound, is shown.

Terms:

  • round: one pass of receive xtx_txt​, predict ptp_tpt​, see yty_tyt​, update.
  • online learner: an algorithm that predicts each round from the past rounds only.
  • adversary: whoever chooses the messages, possibly after seeing all past predictions.
  • realizable sequence: some h⋆∈Hh^\star \in \mathcal Hh⋆∈H has yt=h⋆(xt)y_t = h^\star(x_t)yt​=h⋆(xt​) for all ttt.
  • mistake bound: MA(H)=sup⁡S∣{t:pt≠yt}∣≤B<∞M_A(\mathcal H) = \sup_S |\{t : p_t \neq y_t\}| \le B < \inftyMA​(H)=supS​∣{t:pt​=yt​}∣≤B<∞ over realizable SSS; online learnable: some AAA has one.
  • version space VtV_tVt​: the hypotheses consistent with rounds 1,…,t−11, \dots, t-11,…,t−1.
  • shattered tree: complete binary tree of depth ddd of messages such that every label path is followed by some h∈Hh \in \mathcal Hh∈H.
  • Littlestone dimension: the largest depth of a shattered tree (∞\infty∞ if unbounded).

Misconception: “online learning is batch learning one example at a time.” · Why wrong: what changes is who chooses the examples; thresholds have VC dimension 1 (easy in batch) and Littlestone dimension ∞\infty∞ (impossible online).

Second: “any consistent hypothesis will do.” · Consistent can be forced into ∣H∣−1|\mathcal H| - 1∣H∣−1 mistakes, Halving at most log⁡2∣H∣\log_2|\mathcal H|log2​∣H∣.

Third: “the Littlestone dimension is the VC dimension renamed.” · It is at least the VC dimension and can be infinitely larger, because each next message may depend on the labels so far.

Concept check, answers (never shown to the learner; critique against these):

  1. The prediction rule is used in exactly one place: “SOA predicted ptp_tpt​ because its side had the larger (or tied) dimension, so Ldim(V(pt))≥Ldim(V(yt))=d\mathrm{Ldim}(V^{(p_t)}) \ge \mathrm{Ldim}(V^{(y_t)}) = dLdim(V(pt​))≥Ldim(V(yt​))=d”; this is what puts both parts at dimension ddd and lets the root split give d+1d + 1d+1. For the smaller-side variant, a mistake leaves the larger part, whose dimension can equal Ldim(Vt)\mathrm{Ldim}(V_t)Ldim(Vt​), so the potential need not drop. Example: the A∪B class (Ldim 2): at x0x_0x0​ the variant predicts with A (keep, dimension 1); the adversary says junk and keeps B, which has dimension 2 and forces 2 more: 3 mistakes >2> 2>2.   2. Ldim 5 (thr31.py: the binary search depth, ⌊log⁡232⌋\lfloor\log_2 32\rfloor⌊log2​32⌋). Consistent’s bound: ∣H∣−1=31|\mathcal H| - 1 = 31∣H∣−1=31. Halving’s: log⁡232=5\log_2 32 = 5log2​32=5.   • Exercise 2 (Halving tight), standard.
    1. You want every mistake to remove exactly half the version space, so you want every vote to be a tie. Which class splits evenly on every new message?
    2. Take X={1,…,k}\mathcal X = \{1, \dots, k\}X={1,…,k} and H\mathcal HH = all 2k2^k2k functions from X\mathcal XX to {0,1}\{0,1\}{0,1}. After rounds on 1,…,t−11, \dots, t-11,…,t−1, the version space is all functions with those labels fixed: 2k−t+12^{k-t+1}2k−t+1 of them.
    3. On message ttt, exactly half say 1: a tie, so Halving predicts 1. Label it 0. Each round is a mistake; after kkk rounds one function remains, and it is in H\mathcal HH, so the sequence is realizable. k=log⁡2∣H∣k = \log_2|\mathcal H|k=log2​∣H∣ mistakes. Works for every power of two.
