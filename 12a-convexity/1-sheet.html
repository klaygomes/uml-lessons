Convexity

Teacher's sheet · Lesson text (draft)

Overview

Objective: after this, you can define convex sets, convex functions (chord and epigraph forms), Lipschitzness and smoothness; prove that local minima of convex functions are global, the tangent inequality, the one-variable convexity test, the composition, maximum and sum rules, the Lipschitz composition rule, the quadratic upper bound and self-boundedness of smooth functions, smoothness through a linear function and of an average; say what a convex learning problem is and prove that its ERM problem is convex; and run gradient descent with step 1/β1/\beta1/β on a convex smooth empirical risk, stopping on a certificate ∥∇f(w)∥(∥w∥+B)\|\nabla f(\mathbf w)\|(\|\mathbf w\| + B)∥∇f(w)∥(∥w∥+B) that you can prove, and state its cost.

  • tangent inequality: f(u)≥f(w)+⟨∇f(w),u−w⟩f(\mathbf u) \ge f(\mathbf w) + \langle \nabla f(\mathbf w), \mathbf u - \mathbf w \ranglef(u)≥f(w)+⟨∇f(w),u−w⟩ for convex differentiable fff.

  • certificate: f(w)−f(u)≤∥∇f(w)∥(∥w∥+B)f(\mathbf w) - f(\mathbf u) \le \|\nabla f(\mathbf w)\|(\|\mathbf w\| + B)f(w)−f(u)≤∥∇f(w)∥(∥w∥+B) for all ∥u∥≤B\|\mathbf u\| \le B∥u∥≤B, fff convex and differentiable.

Misconception: "a zero gradient means you have reached the bottom". Why wrong: only for convex functions; the 0-1 risk has zero gradient on every terrace, at 0.8 and at 0.2.

Second: "smooth means convex". Why wrong: the sigmoid loss is smooth and fails the chord test: values 0.982 at z=−4z = -4z=−4 and 0.5 at z=0z = 0z=0, chord midpoint 0.741, curve at z=−2z = -2z=−2 0.8808.

Third: "a bigger step gets there faster, so it is better". Why wrong: 3/β3/\beta3/β happened to work on the logistic risk (27 steps), but the quadratic upper bound allows an increase; on x2x^2x2 it diverges (Problem 1).

Fourth: "a random chord test that finds no violation proves convexity". Why wrong: a test only finds violations; convexity is proved by an argument.

Fifth: "the hinge loss is convex, so the gradient certificate works for it". Why wrong: no gradient at the kink; the act 1 walker's certificate never drops below 0.4271 in 300 steps.
