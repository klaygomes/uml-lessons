Misconception: “the leftovers are a fresh test set, so their error is an unbiased estimate of the true risk.” Why wrong: the kept positions were chosen after seeing every example; for boxes, every leftover junk message lies inside the box by construction. The union over all mkm^kmk lists fixes it at a price of about klog⁡mk\log mklogm.

Second misconception: “the size is the number of distinct kept messages.” Why wrong: the size is the fixed length kkk of the list; the union bound counts lists of length kkk. On the eight examples the list (1,4,1,2)(1, 4, 1, 2)(1,4,1,2) has three distinct messages and k=4k = 4k=4.

Third misconception: “a small certified risk means the bound is close to the truth.” Why wrong: the bound uses only kkk; at m=2,000m = 2{,}000m=2,000 it certifies 0.1695 (the prose rounds to 0.17) while the worst of 1,000 simulated boxes had true risk 0.0062 (0.006). The certificate is a guarantee, not a forecast.

Fourth misconception: “a class with a compression scheme in the realizable case needs a new scheme for noisy data.” Why wrong: for binary labels the upgrade is free (ERM, discard its mistakes, compress the rest), and the bound then compares the true risk with a leftover error that is no longer zero.

Derivation (what the prose proves, in order):

  • Bernstein’s inequality: stated, not proved (as in the book); strict ∣Zi∣<M|Z_i| < M∣Zi​∣<M handled by applying it for every M>1M > 1M>1 and letting M↓1M \downarrow 1M↓1.
  • Fixed-hhh lemma (book B.10): part 1 as the book (variance ≤LD\le L_{\mathcal D}≤LD​ since ℓ2≤ℓ\ell^2 \le \ellℓ2≤ℓ; solve the quadratic; a+b≤a+b\sqrt{a+b} \le \sqrt a + \sqrt ba+b​≤a​+b​; the ≥\ge≥ vs >>> point). Part 2 written out in full: Bernstein on −αi-\alpha_i−αi​, then the quadratic in LD\sqrt{L_{\mathcal D}}LD​​, giving constant 2/3+2+2/3≈3.82<42/3 + 2 + 2/\sqrt3 \approx 3.82 < 42/3+2+2/3​≈3.82<4.

  • Compression bound (book 30.2): fixed III, independence of positions, ∣VI∣≥n|V_I| \ge n∣VI​∣≥n and monotonicity of the slack in ∣V∣|V|∣V∣, union over mkm^kmk lists, learner’s list is one of them, log⁡(mk/δ′)≤klog⁡(m/δ′)\log(m^k/\delta') \le k\log(m/\delta')log(mk/δ′)≤klog(m/δ′), n≥m/2n \ge m/2n≥m/2.
  • Realizable form (book 30.3). Definitions 30.4 and 30.5. Upgrade lemma (book 30.6) with the ERM, discard, compress argument.
  • Boxes: κ\kappaκ keeps extremes, BBB tightest box; consistency from lesson 8.
  • Halfspaces: fold labels (ui=yixi\mathbf u_i = y_i\mathbf x_iui​=yi​xi​); origin not in hull; existence and uniqueness of the nearest point; separation (book’s α\alphaα argument, algebra spelled out); variational inequality ⟨w,p⟩≥∥w∥2\langle \mathbf w, \mathbf p\rangle \ge \|\mathbf w\|^2⟨w,p⟩≥∥w∥2; w\mathbf ww is a convex combination of points on the flat FFF; Carathéodory (stated and proved) in FFF, dimension d−1d - 1d−1, gives ddd points; reconstruction returns w\mathbf ww by containment plus uniqueness.
