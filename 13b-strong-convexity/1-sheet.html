Overview

Misconception (new in the split): "strong convexity is just convexity with a fancier name". Why wrong: convexity allows flat stretches (the valley); strong convexity forces a sag growing with the squared chord length, the floor the stability proof stands on; the penalty supplies it when the loss has none (concept-check 2: a single half square loss in R5\mathbb R^5R5 has σ=0\sigma = 0σ=0).

Second: "more λ\lambdaλ is always safer". Why wrong: the fit rises with λ\lambdaλ for every SSS (proved); the certificate is U-shaped (one-word table: minimum 0.446 near λ=0.1\lambda = 0.1λ=0.1).

Third: "the bound's best λ\lambdaλ is the true best". Why wrong: in the one-word world the bound prefers 0.1 while the true risk prefers the smallest λ\lambdaλ.

Fourth: "these are high-probability guarantees". Why wrong: they bound expectations; Problem 1 (Exercise 1) converts.
