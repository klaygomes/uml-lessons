Misconception: “a flat point might only be a local dip”. Why wrong: for the square loss the expansion adds an average of squares at any zero-gradient point, so every flat point is a global minimum.

Second misconception: “the perfect fit is the best fit”. Why wrong: degree 5 interpolates six points (risk about 10−2010^{-20}10−20) whatever the scores; at x=6x = 6x=6 it predicts 183.0 against the line’s 123.4 and the cubic’s 66.3 (left for build-it step 2, not printed in the prose).

Third misconception: “a logistic probability of 0.71 means 71% of such messages are junk”. Why wrong: only if the data follow the logistic model; calibration is a separate question.
