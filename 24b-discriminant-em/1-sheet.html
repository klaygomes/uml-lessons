Discriminant Analysis and EM

Overview

Objective: after this, you can describe a Gaussian cloud in ddd dimensions by its centre and covariance matrix; derive that under the LDA assumptions the Bayes optimal predictor is a halfspace and give the costs of LDA; define a latent variable and a mixture of Gaussians; define EM, prove it is alternate maximization of GGG and that it never decreases the likelihood; write soft k-means and its cost; show why a hard assignment loses the guarantee (L=G+τL = G + \tauL=G+τ, the rounding tax, and the chain L(θ(t+1))≥L(θ(t))−τ+γL(\theta^{(t+1)}) \ge L(\theta^{(t)}) - \tau + \gammaL(θ(t+1))≥L(θ(t))−τ+γ); explain why identical centres never separate; and certify an EM run (exact E step, M step that never lowers FFF, settled, close to the truth).

Misconception: “EM finds the maximum likelihood estimate”. Why wrong: only monotone; local maxima; identical starting centres never separate (fixed point: Qi,y=cyQ_{i,y} = c_yQi,y​=cy​, both centres go to the overall average).

Second: “a hard assignment is just a cheaper soft one”. Why wrong: GGG no longer touches LLL; a round promises only L−τ+γL - \tau + \gammaL−τ+γ; on the test archive the loop fell from −422.2-422.2−422.2 to −433.7-433.7−433.7 while the soft loop climbed to −364.0-364.0−364.0.

Derivation:

  • LDA: log-likelihood ratio, expansion with symmetry of Σ−1\Sigma^{-1}Σ−1, Equation 24.8; b=−12⟨w,μ1+μ0⟩b = -\frac12\langle w, \mu_1 + \mu_0\rangleb=−21​⟨w,μ1​+μ0​⟩.
  • EM: mixture density, log-likelihood with the sum inside the log, FFF, E and M steps; Lemma (alternate maximization of GGG) with Jensen replaced by log⁡t≤t−1\log t \le t - 1logt≤t−1 per message; Theorem (monotone) as a chain of two equalities and two inequalities.
  • Hard assignment (ours): for a one-hot row the entropy term of GGG is 0, so message iii’s part of GGG is log⁡Pθ[xi,y^i]=log⁡Pθ[xi]+log⁡max⁡yPθ[Y=y∣xi]\log\mathbb P_\theta[x_i, \hat y_i] = \log\mathbb P_\theta[x_i] + \log\max_y\mathbb P_\theta[Y = y\mid x_i]logPθ​[xi​,y^​i​]=logPθ​[xi​]+logmaxy​Pθ​[Y=y∣xi​], giving L=G(Qhard,θ)+τL = G(Q^{\mathrm{hard}},\theta) + \tauL=G(Qhard,θ)+τ. With γ\gammaγ the gain of the M step in G(Qhard,⋅)G(Q^{\mathrm{hard}}, \cdot)G(Qhard,⋅) (nonnegative because the old parameters are a candidate) and G≤LG \le LG≤L for every QQQ: L(θ(t+1))≥G(Qhard,θ(t+1))=G(Qhard,θ(t))+γ=L(θ(t))−τ+γL(\theta^{(t+1)}) \ge G(Q^{\mathrm{hard}},\theta^{(t+1)}) = G(Q^{\mathrm{hard}},\theta^{(t)}) + \gamma = L(\theta^{(t)}) - \tau + \gammaL(θ(t+1))≥G(Qhard,θ(t+1))=G(Qhard,θ(t))+γ=L(θ(t))−τ+γ. Soft: τ=0\tau = 0τ=0.
  • Identical centres (ours): equal bells cancel in Bayes’ rule, Qi,y=cyQ_{i,y} = c_yQi,y​=cy​; M step gives μy=∑icyxi/∑icy\mu_y = \sum_i c_y x_i/\sum_i c_yμy​=∑i​cy​xi​/∑i​cy​ = overall average, cyc_ycy​ unchanged; a fixed point.
  • Soft k-means: E step with ZiZ_iZi​, M step objective 24.13, μy\mu_yμy​ by gradient (concave quadratic), cyc_ycy​ stated (Problem 1).
  • Safe M steps (sheet only, not in the prose; the lab’s certificate part 1 uses it): for fixed QQQ the M-step objective is a sum of a concave function of the shares (∑yνylog⁡cy\sum_y\nu_y\log c_y∑y​νy​logcy​) and a concave quadratic in the centres, so it is concave; the half-way point between the old parameters and the maximizer has FFF at least the average of the two values, hence at least the old FFF. Centres only: maximizing over a subset that contains the old values never lowers FFF. Jitter can lower FFF. All three “safe” variants satisfy the lemma’s requirement (concept check 1: only “at least as good as θ(t)\theta^{(t)}θ(t)” is used).
